Dam Rules (දම් නීති)

1. Movement (ඉත්තන් ඇදීම)

Regular pieces move diagonally forward one square onto empty dark squares.

2. Mandatory Capture (අනිවාර්ය කැපීම)

If you can jump over an opponent's piece into an empty square behind it, you must capture it. Multi-jumps in a single turn are also mandatory!

3. Flying King (රජ වීම)

Reach the opponent's back row to become a King. Following Sri Lankan/International rules, Kings can slide any number of empty squares diagonally forward or backward, and capture from a distance!

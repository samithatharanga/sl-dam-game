**Sri Lankan & International Dam (Draughts)** is a free, zero-install browser game built with HTML5, CSS3, and vanilla JavaScript. It supports **Sri Lankan and International Dam rules**—including **Flying Kings**, **mandatory captures**, and **multi-jump chains**—on both **8×8** and **10×10** boards, playable in **Pass & Play (2 Players)** or **vs. AI Bot (Easy, Medium, Hard)** across Sri Lanka, South Asia, and worldwide.

## Quick Answers (AEO — Frequently Asked Questions)

### What is Sri Lankan Dam (Draughts)?
Sri Lankan Dam (commonly called *Dam* in Sri Lanka and *Draughts* or *Checkers* internationally) is a diagonal strategy board game played on dark squares of an **8×8** or **10×10** board where players capture opposing pieces by jumping over them and promote pieces reaching the back row into **Flying Kings** that can slide across multiple open diagonal squares.

### Is this Dam game free to play?
Yes. **Sri Lankan & International Dam** is 100% free to play directly in web browsers on desktop, laptop, tablet, and mobile devices with no sign-up, paywall, or download required.

### Does it support Flying Kings and mandatory captures?
Yes. Crowned pieces (**Kings**) move diagonally forward and backward across any number of empty squares and perform long-range captures, while capture moves are enforced with visual highlights and automatic multi-jump continuation.

## Core Gameplay & Features

- **Sri Lankan & International Ruleset:**
  - **Flying Kings:** Promoted pieces can slide diagonally across multiple open squares (forward and backward) and capture from afar.
  - **Mandatory Captures:** Capture opportunities are highlighted and enforced according to traditional Dam rules.
  - **Multi-Jump Chains:** When a piece captures an opponent piece and can continue jumping, the turn stays locked to that piece until the chain completes.
- **8×8 & 10×10 Board Options:** Switch between **8×8 (12 pieces per side)** and **10×10 (20 pieces per side)** boards.
- **Game Modes:**
  - **Pass & Play (2 Players):** Play locally with a friend on one screen.
  - **AI Opponent:** Play solo against an AI bot with **Easy**, **Medium**, and **Hard** (Minimax with Alpha-Beta pruning) difficulty levels.
- **Web & Audio Experience:** Built-in synthesized sound effects with a mute toggle, responsive layout, and static hosting compatibility on GitHub Pages, Vercel, and Netlify.
